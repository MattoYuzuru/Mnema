# Greek gods companion illustration provenance

Created 2026-10-09 with the built-in image-generation tool at the owner's request.
The owner selected Apollo, Ares, Hermes and Zeus; the already-created
[Apollo](./apollo-engraving.png) is reused (see [its provenance](./apollo-provenance.md)).

New outputs, all opaque RGB PNGs at 1024 × 1536 pixels:

- [Ares](./ares-engraving.png): crested helmet, spear, cuirass and mantle.
- [Hermes](./hermes-engraving.png): winged cap, caduceus and drapery.
- [Zeus](./zeus-engraving.png): mature bearded figure, thunderbolt bundle and drapery.

Each generation used [homepage Mnemosyne](../../../frontend/src/assets/brand/mnemosyne-1024.png)
as the primary style and palette reference, with Apollo as the accepted companion.
These are original generated raster illustrations, not photographs of documented
ancient statues or vector assets. They are design masters, not connected to the homepage.

Visually checked for cream/indigo engraved linework, blank marble eyes, distinct
attributes and consistent portrait framing. Color matching is visual; no claim of
identical per-pixel background color is made. No hand-written image processing was used.

## Ares generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story.
Create one NEW original Greek god illustration for a matching Mnema portrait series. Image 1 (Mnemosyne) is the primary STYLE AND PALETTE REFERENCE; image 2 (Apollo) shows the accepted companion in the same series. Neither is an edit target.
Match the same antique copperplate engraving of classical marble statuary: warm cream ivory figure approximately #FBF5DE, extremely fine deep indigo contour lines, curved hatching, cross-hatching in the shadows, subtle warm paper texture inside ivory shapes. Restrained two-color print. Sculpted eyes WITHOUT pupils. Serene harmonious dignified face, precise anatomy and elegant flowing classical drapery.
Opaque continuous deep indigo background, intended #251079 with reference dominant pixels near #240E7D, visually identical to the references. At most the reference's very subtle ink grain. No scenery, gradient, vignette, glow, clouds or frame.
Portrait 2:3, 1024x1536 if possible. Single standing figure from head to upper thighs, drapery cropped at the bottom. Similar head size and figure scale to the references, head completely within frame with a small top margin. Subtle three-quarter view.
No text, captions, logo, watermark, modern objects, photorealism, 3D rendering, painterly shading or additional colors. It should look like the same engraver created all portraits.
Subject: ARES, the Greek god of war. A distinct adult masculine athletic classical statue with a strong jaw, short curly hair, a clean-shaven face and a composed resolute expression. Wear an elegant Greek crested helmet tipped back enough to expose the full face; crest and helmet entirely within the canvas. A restrained classical cuirass visible beneath a flowing mantle. One hand holds a slender upright spear beside the body, the other rests naturally. Ivory helmet and armor use exactly the same fine indigo engraved treatment, with no metal colors. No battle scene or aggression. The spear remains a secondary identifying attribute; the face and drapery are dominant.
